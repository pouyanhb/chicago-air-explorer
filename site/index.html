METHODS & TRANSPARENCY

What these readings mean.

The design prioritizes transparent comparisons over regulatory claims or source attribution.

01 / SOURCES

Published data, not the live Clarity feed

Historical daily summaries are generated from archived hourly measurements, with later updates taken from the City's Open Air Chicago Hour Aggregations. This public source can lag behind measurement time. Displayed timestamps refer to observations, not download time.

02 / AVERAGING

Daily time uses UTC

One UTC calendar day means 24 hours, not a Chicago-local day. For each pollutant and sensor, a daily mean is shown only with at least 18 valid hourly readings. Network means average eligible sensor-day values equally, and report how many sensors contributed.

03 / MATCHING

Compare like with like

For multi-sensor comparisons, only dates with a valid reading from every selected sensor for that pollutant are retained. The comparison table reports the number of shared days. PM2.5–NO₂ scatterplots pair both pollutants within the same sensor and UTC day.

04 / LIMITATIONS

Not a regulatory determination

These are non-regulatory sensor readings. The 18-of-24-hour criterion is an exploratory completeness rule, not a regulatory design-value procedure. The data do not establish compliance, personal exposure, or pollution sources. Missing hours and pollutants are never filled with zero.

05 / REVISIONS

Updated automatically

A scheduled GitHub Action checks a limited number of recent hourly UTC days and updates the public summaries. The historical archive is not re-downloaded each time. Corrections to much older observations may not be captured automatically, and delays are identified by measurement timestamps.

06 / INDEPENDENCE

Public-data project

This independent website is not operated or endorsed by Open Air Chicago, Clarity, or the City of Chicago. For immediate public-health conditions, use official guidance and monitoring services. The values shown here may be delayed or incomplete.
